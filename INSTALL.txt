Dual AI Chat - Installation guide
=================================

Ask Claude and ChatGPT (Codex) the same question, see both answers side by side, and get an
automatic scored comparison with a visual report. It uses your own subscriptions - no per-use
billing - and every chat stays on your own computer.

(中文说明见 INSTALL.zh-CN.txt / 日本語の手順は INSTALL.ja.txt)


WHAT YOU NEED
- A Windows 10 or 11 PC with an internet connection
- A Claude subscription (Pro or Max)
- A ChatGPT subscription that includes Codex (Plus / Pro, etc.)
- Nothing else to install beforehand, and no admin rights


INSTALL (about 5-15 minutes depending on your connection)
1. Download dual-ai-chat-v<version>.zip from the GitHub Releases page and extract it to a
   permanent place, e.g. D:\dual-ai-chat
   Note: use "Extract All" first - do not run it from inside the zip.

2. Open the extracted dual-ai-chat folder and double-click install.bat.
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
   - It downloads Node.js, Claude Code and Codex into this folder's runtime\ subfolder.
     Nothing is installed system-wide.

3. A browser opens twice so you can sign in:
   - First: sign in to your Claude subscription account and approve access.
   - Second: choose "Sign in with ChatGPT" and sign in to your ChatGPT account.
   After each sign-in, go back to the black window - it continues automatically.

4. It asks about two optional features. Pressing Enter (Y) for both is recommended:
   - PDF / Word / Excel reading (~60 MB)
   - iPhone photo (HEIC) support (~100 MB)

5. When you see "All done!", it opens the app. A "Dual AI Chat" icon appears on your desktop.


EVERYDAY USE
- Open: double-click "Dual AI Chat" on the desktop (or start.vbs in the folder).
- Ask: choose "Claude only", "Codex only" or "Both · compare", then press Enter.
- Attachments: drag images, screenshots, iPhone photos, PDF, Word, Excel or CSV into the window,
  or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V.
- History: browse past chats by date (with a calendar) or by topic on the left, or search.
- Language: switch between 中文 / 日本語 / English at the bottom left.


TROUBLESHOOTING
- Something failed or the network dropped: double-click install.bat again. It only adds what
  is missing. The install log is data\install.log.
- "Not signed in" / login expired: double-click install.bat again to reopen the sign-in pages.
- Stop the background service: "Settings" (bottom left) -> "Stop background service".
- Uninstall: stop the background service, then delete the whole folder and the desktop icon.
  (Sign-in data lives in C:\Users\<you>\.claude and .codex - delete those too if you like.)


PRIVACY
Chats, attachments and reports are stored only in this folder's data\ subfolder and are not
uploaded anywhere else (your questions are, of course, sent to Claude / ChatGPT to be answered).
