★ Combined best answer Merges the strengths of both and fixes their mistakes

  1. Show total costs early. Surprise shipping fees and taxes are among the top reasons shoppers leave. Show them, or a shipping estimator, on the cart page. A free-shipping threshold can also help.
  2. Simplify checkout. Offer guest checkout, keep form fields to a minimum, enable autofill, and add express pay options (Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal).
  3. Recover abandoned carts. Send automated email or SMS reminders, for example at 1 hour, 24 hours and 72 hours, with a cart preview and a direct link back. You can test a small incentive in the last message.

💡 How to use this

Start by showing total costs early and adding express checkout. Then set up automated reminders, and offer incentives sparingly so customers don't learn to abandon carts on purpose to get a discount.

Three high-impact fixes:

  1. Show total costs early. Unexpected shipping fees, taxes, and surcharges are the #1 reason shoppers bail. Display them (or a shipping estimator) on the product or cart page, and consider a free-shipping threshold.
  2. Simplify checkout. Offer guest checkout, cut form fields to the essentials, enable autofill, and add express payment options (Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Shop Pay). Fewer steps mean fewer exits.
  3. Recover abandoned carts. Send an automated email/SMS sequence (e.g., 1 hour, 24 hours, 72 hours) with a cart preview and a direct link back. Optionally add a small incentive in the last message.
